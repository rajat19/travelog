## The Harbor City

> Sydney is at its best when you stop treating the harbor as a backdrop and let it become the route.

Sydney is a city of big first impressions: the Opera House, the Harbour Bridge, ferries pulling away from Circular Quay, and the water catching light from every angle. But the city becomes more memorable when you leave the postcard view and follow its edges.

<Highlight title="Must-See Sydney">
- **Circular Quay to the Opera House**: The classic arrival walk, especially around golden hour.
- **Sydney Harbour Bridge**: Walk across for skyline views without needing the full BridgeClimb.
- **Bondi to Coogee**: The coastal walk that makes beach life feel like a civic ritual.
</Highlight>

<Tip title="Ferry First">
Take a public ferry early in the trip, ideally to Manly. It is cheaper than a harbor cruise and gives you the same cinematic sense of the city from the water.
</Tip>

## Neighborhood Rhythm

The city rewards moving by mood. Start central around Circular Quay and The Rocks, then use ferries and trains to branch out. Surry Hills is excellent for cafes and dinner, Newtown brings a more alternative edge, and the eastern beaches shift the day into saltwater mode.

## The Food Scene

Sydney eating is shaped by immigration, seafood, brunch culture, and neighborhoods that each have their own flavor. Plan at least one meal around the water and one around a suburb away from the icons.

<FoodGuide>
| Dish | Where to Try | Price |
|------|--------------|-------|
| **Flat White & Brunch** | Surry Hills or Darlinghurst cafes | ~A$20-35 |
| **Seafood** | Sydney Fish Market or harbor restaurants | ~A$25-60 |
| **Thai / Malaysian / Vietnamese** | Newtown, Haymarket, or Chinatown | ~A$18-35 |
| **Gelato** | Inner-city dessert stops | ~A$8-12 |
</FoodGuide>

<Itinerary>
- **Day 1: Harbor Icons** — Circular Quay -> Opera House -> Royal Botanic Garden -> The Rocks at night.
- **Day 2: Beaches** — Bondi breakfast -> Bondi to Coogee walk -> swim and sunset drinks.
- **Day 3: Ferry Day** — Manly ferry -> beach walk -> return to Barangaroo or Darling Harbour.
- **Day 4: Neighborhoods** — Surry Hills cafes -> Newtown browsing -> dinner in Chinatown or Potts Point.
</Itinerary>

## Logistics

<GettingThere>
Sydney Airport (SYD) is close to the city. The Airport Link train is usually the easiest route into Central, Circular Quay, or Town Hall, and contactless cards work across most public transport.
</GettingThere>

<Accommodation title="Where I Would Stay">
**Surry Hills or Darlinghurst**: Central enough for transit, lively for food, and more local-feeling than staying directly on the harbor. For a first visit focused on views, Circular Quay or The Rocks is convenient but noticeably pricier.
</Accommodation>
