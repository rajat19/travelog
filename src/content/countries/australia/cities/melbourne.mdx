## The Laneway Capital

> Melbourne does not announce itself all at once. It wins you over in smaller rituals: a perfect coffee, a tram bell, a gallery hour, a narrow lane full of murals.

Melbourne is quieter than Sydney at first glance, but it has a deep urban texture. The city center is compact, trams make it easy to drift between neighborhoods, and the best days are built from cafes, markets, galleries, and late dinners rather than one giant landmark.

<Highlight title="Must-See Melbourne">
- **Flinders Street Station**: The classic meeting point and a perfect orientation marker.
- **Hosier Lane**: A quick hit of street art and laneway energy near Federation Square.
- **National Gallery of Victoria**: One of the best free cultural anchors in the city.
</Highlight>

<Tip title="Cafe Timing">
Many of Melbourne's best cafes are breakfast-and-lunch focused. Go early, treat coffee as an event, and do not save the good cafe for late afternoon.
</Tip>

## Neighborhood Rhythm

The CBD is useful, but Melbourne opens up in its neighborhoods. Fitzroy and Collingwood bring vintage shops, bars, murals, and excellent food. Carlton is built for Italian dinners. Southbank gives skyline views along the Yarra, while St Kilda adds a seaside change of pace.

## The Food Scene

Melbourne is one of the easiest cities to eat well without a rigid plan. Coffee is the obvious headline, but the city is also excellent for Italian, Greek, Vietnamese, modern Australian, bakeries, wine bars, and market snacks.

<FoodGuide>
| Dish | Where to Try | Price |
|------|--------------|-------|
| **Flat White** | CBD, Fitzroy, or Collingwood cafes | ~A$5-7 |
| **Market Snacks** | Queen Victoria Market | ~A$10-25 |
| **Italian Dinner** | Carlton | ~A$25-50 |
| **Modern Australian Brunch** | Fitzroy or South Melbourne | ~A$22-40 |
</FoodGuide>

<Itinerary>
- **Day 1: City Core** — Flinders Street -> Federation Square -> NGV -> Southbank/Yarra walk.
- **Day 2: Laneways & Markets** — Coffee crawl -> Hosier Lane -> Queen Victoria Market -> Chinatown dinner.
- **Day 3: Northside** — Fitzroy and Collingwood shops, murals, cafes, and wine bars.
- **Day 4: Coastal Reset** — St Kilda pier and beach -> sunset -> relaxed final dinner.
</Itinerary>

## Logistics

<GettingThere>
Melbourne Airport (MEL) is connected to the CBD by SkyBus and rideshare. Within the city, trams are the signature mode; the central Free Tram Zone is useful, but you will need a Myki card for wider travel.
</GettingThere>

<Accommodation title="Where I Would Stay">
**CBD, Fitzroy, or Southbank**: Stay in the CBD for first-visit convenience, Fitzroy for food and nightlife, or Southbank for river views and easy gallery access.
</Accommodation>
